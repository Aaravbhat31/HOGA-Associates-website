About HOGA Associates

At HOGA Associates Pvt. Ltd., we bridge the gap between visionary industry leaders and high-caliber corporate talent. Driven by our core philosophy—Passion to Excel—we specialize in executive search, specialized recruitment, and strategic HR consulting for core industrial sectors across India.

Headquartered with a strong footprint across Northern India and operating pan-India, we understand the distinct talent requirements of modern manufacturing, textile, and technical operations. From placing C-suite leadership like IT Project Heads and Supply Chain Management (SCM) executives to assembling specialized engineering teams, we deliver hiring solutions that drive operational growth.

Our deep industry roots, combined with a rigorous talent-mapping methodology, ensure that we don't just fill vacancies—we secure leaders who align with your corporate culture and operational goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity for corporate clients and candidates seeking executive recruitment services.

What is HOGA's typical turnaround time for executive placement?

For specialized mid-to-senior roles, we deliver shortlisted candidate profiles within 48 to 72 hours thanks to our deep pan-India talent database. Executive headhunting searches typically complete within 2 to 3 weeks.

Are there any fees for job seekers to register or apply?

No, candidate registration and CV submission are 100% free. We operate purely on an employer-retained and placement fee model with corporate clients.

Which geographic regions in India do you cover?

We provide executive recruitment and staffing services all across India. While we have deep operational roots and strong talent networks across Northern India's major industrial belts (including Punjab, Haryana, Ludhiana, Panipat, and the NCR region), we manage mid-to-senior leadership searches for corporate clients nationwide.

How does HOGA handle candidate and corporate confidentiality?

We maintain strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Sensitive senior leadership replacements are conducted with utmost privacy, ensuring candidate identities are protected until formal interview stages.
